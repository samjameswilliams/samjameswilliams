>$^{39}$You search the Scriptures because you think that in them you have eternal life; and it is they that bear witness about me, $^{40}$yet you refuse to come to me that you may have life.

These words of Jesus are particularly searching for me. It is so much easier for a proud person to study and strive to gain knowledge about scripture than to come to Jesus. Even though scripture points us to Jesus this is true. Jesus' choice of words is careful and deliberate here. Jesus is not looking for people who "search the scripture" in the sense that they make it their subject. Jesus is looking for people who love the scriptures and become subject to it because they love Jesus and want to have a close relationship with him. Scripture itself doesn't save us, only Jesus can.

>$^{41}$I do not receive glory from people.

The essence of pride is finding a sense of value in the praise and esteem of people. Jesus himself sets an example by rejecting that motivation. Jesus is only concerned with receiving glory from the Father. 

$^{44}$How can you believe, when you receive glory from one another and do not seek the glory that comes from the only God?

Jesus is particularly addressing the religious leaders around him but the warnings apply in other situations. Where Jesus says they "receive glory from one another", there are many types of groups that do this, rewarding people for the things the group value. This makes it very easy to be motivated by esteem and reputation rather than by wanting to do the right thing. This can involve fooling people into thinking you're not doing something you are or are doing something you're not, slandering another person to be seen as better than them, excluding people who may be better than you at something or taking credit for other's achievements. 

Even if it involves none of these underhand tactics, Jesus asks "how can you believe"? He's saying that receiving glory from people is a direct obstacle to belief in him. This should make us very wary indeed of pride growing in our hearts. Pride is so often talked about as a positive thing but it is terrible. The proud person feels no need for Jesus, and even studying scripture which talks about our need for Jesus so clearly won't save them.
